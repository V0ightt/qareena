## Data

- **Synthetic only.** Fictional identities and businesses are generated by an LLM and Faker from our own schema. Documents are rendered from our own templates, which are clearly marked SPECIMEN and use ICAO's fictional issuer "UTO".
- No real identity documents, customer data or personal data are used, ours or anyone else's.
- Physical photos are of printed **synthetic** documents only.
