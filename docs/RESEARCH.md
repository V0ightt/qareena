# Research notes and sources (22 Oct 2026)

These sources informed the design. **✔** means the specific claim was checked against the source page while preparing the design brief. **○** means it was gathered by research assistants and not re-checked yet. Re-check ○ items before quoting them publicly. *Preprint* means not peer-reviewed.

## Models and OCR on Arabic

- ✔ **arXiv 2610.25235**, *Evaluating Cloud and Self-Hosted OCR Systems for Arabic, English, and French Documents* (18 Oct 2026, preprint). Arabic CER, print (119 pages) / handwriting (10 pages):

  | System | Print | Handwriting |
  |---|---|---|
  | Datalab | 0.129 | 0.170 |
  | Azure DI | 0.142 | 0.171 |
  | Document AI | 0.139 | 0.177 |
  | Surya | 0.203 | 0.251 |
  | PaddleOCR-VL | 0.326 | 0.444 |
  | Qwen3-VL | 0.363 | 0.504 |
  | Tesseract | 0.368 | 0.759 |

  The paper also rates Qwen3-VL boxes "Poor" on 12 of 14 Arabic/French pages. Caveat: the reference transcriptions were generated by Gemini. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25235
- ✔ **Qwen3.5-4B model card**: natively multimodal; Apache-2.0; OCRBench 85.0; OmniDocBench1.5 86.2; thinks by default and can be switched off with `enable_thinking=False`. https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B
- ○ **KITAB-Bench** (Arabic OCR benchmark, 2025): Gemini-2.0-Flash CER 0.13; AIN-7B 0.20; Tesseract 0.54. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14949
- ○ PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 multilingual (Arabic group): https://www.paddleocr.ai/main/en/version3.x/algorithm/PP-OCRv5/PP-OCRv5_multi_languages.html
- ○ Ollama logprobs (since v0.12.11): https://github.com/ollama/ollama/releases/tag/v0.12.11. The UTF-8 partial-token issue is #13497.
- ○ Gemini models, pricing and free-tier data terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models · https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing

## Confidence, calibration, evaluation

- ✔ **arXiv 2609.20110**, *Perception, Layout, and Validation: Calibrated Confidence for Reliable Straight-Through Processing of Financial Documents* (17 Sep 2026, preprint). Reports AUROC 0.54–0.74 for verbalised VLM signals vs 0.90–0.99 for the decomposed score.
- ○ arXiv 2606.24420 (DocILE field confidence: logprob, verbalised and self-consistency vs fused classifiers; preprint).
- ○ Microsoft Content Understanding, on confidence, grounding and human-in-the-loop: https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/content-understanding-confidence-grounding-hitl/
- ○ Azure Document Intelligence, accuracy and confidence: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/concept/accuracy-confidence
- ○ scikit-learn calibration guide (isotonic regression needs more than ~1,000 samples): https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html
- ○ Learn-Then-Test (arXiv 2110.01052); selective classification (arXiv 1705.08500); calibration error estimation (arXiv 1909.10155, 1904.01685); Wilson intervals (Brown, Cai & DasGupta 2001).

- ○ Augraphy: https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14558 · MIDV-2020 (mock IDs printed and laminated): https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00396
